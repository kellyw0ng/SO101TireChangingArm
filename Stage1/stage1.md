# Stage 1

This Stage includes setting up the wheel test corner (skip if have 1:10 RC car), installing the 3D printed hex socket onto motor 5, counting the number of turns to tighten and loosen the nut on the wheel.

---
## 3D Print Settings for the Test Corner

Material: PLA+

Find the 3D stl files under folder "stl"

test_corner_stand.stl: print it as it loads, inner face down. 15–20% infill is fine.
test_corner_wheel.stl: Use 30% or more infill and 4 walls so the upright stays stiff.

---

## Putting together the test corner

- The M4 × 30 mm bolt goes in from the back of the stand. 
- The wheel slides onto that hub. 
- The bolt comes through the hole in the middle of the wheel. 
- The M4 x 7.2 mm nut threads onto the bolt from the front, inside the well with the four posts.
